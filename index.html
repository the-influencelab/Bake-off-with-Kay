This week's recipe

Secret Banana Bread

You'll need

  • Organic oat flour about 200 g
  • Three eggs
  • Two and a half bananas
  • Vanilla essence I use organic vanilla
  • A splash of baking powder
  • Cinnamon
  • A tiny bit of salt

For the topping, per slice

  • A tiny bit of coconut oil
  • Manuka honey
  • Four cranberries
  • Five blueberries
  • Two dates
  1. Heat the ovenPre heat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Mix bit by bitMash the bananas, then add the eggs, vanilla, oat flour, baking powder, cinnamon and salt, mixing bit by bit until smooth.
  3. BakePour into a lined loaf tin and pop in the oven for forty minutes.
  4. Make the toppingWhen it comes out, get a pan and pop in a tiny bit of coconut oil with the manuka honey. Add four cranberries, five blueberries and two dates, and warm through.
  5. ServeCut a slice and spoon the warm fruit over the top.

Pop the rest in the fridge and voila, you have a weekly breakfast or a convenient snack.
